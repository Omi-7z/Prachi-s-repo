Karigar · The Ludo Mandala

Every craft has a story. The maker should be the one telling it.

Karigar gives artisans a simple board of questions about their craft, their place and the people who taught them. They answer in their own voice and language, then decide, story by story, who gets to hear it.

How a story is made

From a conversation on the floor to a page the maker controls.

1
A facilitator brings the board

Someone from a local NGO or cooperative sits with the artisan at home or in the workshop. There are no forms, only the board and a phone.

2
The artisan answers out loud

Each dot is a question card. They answer by voice, in Gujarati, Marathi, Hindi or whatever they speak. Any card can be skipped and come back to later.

3
Karigar drafts, they correct

Their answers become a draft story in their own words. Nothing gets polished into someone else’s voice, and every line can be changed.

4
They choose who sees it

Only me, people I approve, anyone with the link, or everyone. It is set for each story and can be changed in one tap at any time.

The Craft Voice Kit

Six decks of cards. Each one is a ring of the board.

The board comes with printed cards, in English and Hindi. The artisan draws a card, the facilitator reads it aloud, and the artisan answers in their own words. Draw one to see what they are asked.

The Craft Voice Kit

Cards an artisan can hold. Questions they answer out loud.

One board and six theme decks, plus reflection cards and small story cards for the end of a session. Everything is printed in English and Hindi. Every answer the artisan gives goes into their own Karigar record.

How a session uses the kit

Start at the centre of the board and work outwards.

1Lay out the boardRead the consent script aloud first. Then put each deck beside its ring.
2Draw from one deckEach deck has three themes with three prompts each. Begin with My Craft.
3Answer out loudThe prompt in quotes helps if the question feels too big. Any card can be skipped.
4Close with reflectionName one strength, one concern, what may be shared, and one hope for the future.

Data commitments

  • Deleted from live systems within 7 days, and from backups within 35 days.
  • Full export in open formats — media at original size, consent and boundary records included.
  • Photo location data is stripped on upload.
  • Nothing is used for training, marketing or licensing without separate, specific consent.

What we can’t promise

Credit lines, rights data and “no AI” instructions are built into every page and image. A determined person can still strip them. Karigar makes correct credit the default and stripping it a deliberate act — it cannot make copying impossible.

The facilitator console helps NGOs, cooperatives and field teams run Karigar card sessions, turn recordings into an artisan’s own record, and hand it over. What happens after that belongs to the artisan.
