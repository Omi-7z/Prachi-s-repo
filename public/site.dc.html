How it works

A conversation on the floor becomes a page the maker controls.

At the heart of every session is the Mandala board: six rings of questions, from the craft itself out to what the maker is proud of.

See a full session →
01 · Before the session A facilitator brings the kit and reads the consent script first.
02 · The Mandala board The artisan draws a card and answers out loud, in their own language. 18 cards and 56 questions over six rings. Start at the centre, skip anything, come back another day. Tap a dot to draw its card.
{{ r.l }}
03 · After the session Karigar drafts the story in their words. They correct it until it sounds like them.
My grandmother tied three hundred nine hundred knots for one odhni. Corrected by Meena · 2 Nov · the change is kept in the story's history
04 · Their choice They decide who hears it, one story at a time.
