How it works

From a card on the floor to a story the maker owns.

1
A session, in person

A facilitator sits with the artisan and a mandala board of cards — Place, Lineage, Material, Culture, Pride. The artisan answers in their own language. Nothing is written for them.

2
Their own app

The artisan receives a link or QR card for their Karigar app: the session, drafts in their voice, and a guide to practise talking about their work. The organisation’s access ends at upload.

3
Publishing, by choice

Only the artisan can publish. For each story they pick who can see it, for what purpose, and for how long. Before confirming, Karigar says in plain words — and out loud — who will see it.

4
Changeable, always

Correct a fact, close a story, go fully private, or take everything and leave. None of it needs anyone’s permission.

Four ways to share a story

Set per story, and within a story per detail — a maker can publish how they work while keeping their village and their material source private. Every story starts as “Only me”.

Data commitments

  • Deleted from live systems within 7 days, and from backups within 35 days.
  • Full export in open formats — media at original size, consent and boundary records included.
  • Photo location data is stripped on upload.
  • Nothing is used for training, marketing or licensing without separate, specific consent.

What we can’t promise

Credit lines, rights data and “no AI” instructions are built into every page and image. A determined person can still strip them. Karigar makes correct credit the default and stripping it a deliberate act — it cannot make copying impossible.

For organisations

Facilitate the session. Then step back.

The facilitator console helps NGOs, cooperatives and field teams run Karigar card sessions, turn recordings into an artisan’s own record, and hand it over. What happens after that belongs to the artisan.

Sign in to the console Request an account
Your organisation can
  • Create artisan records and run card sessions
  • Upload recordings and session notes
  • Review the AI-drafted profile with the artisan before handover
  • Send each artisan their app by link or printed QR card
  • Help an artisan use their account while sitting with them — every assisted action is logged
Your organisation cannot
  • Publish, edit or take down anything for an artisan
  • See an artisan’s private archive or practice conversations
  • Read or answer their contact requests
  • Use their stories in your own reports or campaigns without their separate consent

This is enforced by permissions, not by trust.

Before a first session

Read the consent script aloud, in the artisan’s language, before the cards come out. It includes one line facilitators often skip:

“When you practise conversations in your app, what you say is processed by Google’s Gemini service to write the replies. It is not published, and you can delete it.”

Then confirm the artisan has the app working on their own phone before you leave.
